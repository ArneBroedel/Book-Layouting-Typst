## Defaults (flexible)

1. Free vision = **one focused visual claim**; **no labels in raster** by default.  
2. Typst owns German claim text.  
3. Illustration-semantics gate is hard (laterality, leaders, structure).  
4. FAIL → demote to **simple alternative** (clinical table/cluster) — never placeholder geometry.  
5. Density: anti-sprawl **and** anti-squish; page count is not KPI.  
6. Solid workable > pretty wrong.
