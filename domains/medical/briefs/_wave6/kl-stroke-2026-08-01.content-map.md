## What this chapter teaches

1. **Gatekeeper:** Schlaganfall/TIA are **not** PT/HP acute working diagnoses → suspicion = triage, usually **112**.
2. **FAST ≥ 1** with sudden onset → Notruf **without** delaying tests.
3. **TIA** (even remitted) = emergency until exclusion; Remission entwarnt **nicht**.
4. **„Last seen normal“** document and hand off (Thrombolysefenster typ. < 4,5 h).
5. **Posterior gap:** FAST alone insensitive for posterior/brainstem; BE-FAST sensibilizes, **does not** replace FAST-112 logic.
6. **Four false frames** (Bell / HWS / Migräne-Aura / Schulter-Arm) → central danger; Unsicherheit prioritizes dangerous diagnosis.
7. **SCOPE:** no ischemia vs bleeding rule-out; no Lyse/Thrombektomie; ABCD² informative only; no HWS-Manipulation / Provokationstests.
