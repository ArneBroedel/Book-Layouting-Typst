## What this chapter teaches

1. **Recognize** silent bone loss → fragility fracture (Bagatell, Bücken, Heben) as clinical reveal.
2. **Do not mislabel** osteoporotic WK-fracture as „Block“ / Lumbago; Prellung after Bagatell as harmless.
3. **Triage:** T1 112 (neuro compression at WK-fracture suspicion **and/or** fall + anticoagulation + head trauma) ≠ T2 NA/gleichtags (WK-fracture suspicion after Bagatell/Bücken; inability to load extremity; Bagatell fracture) ≠ T4 treat-with-net (stable, no RF).
4. **Stop payload:** no forced flexion, no HVT/impulse manipulation at unclear WK status; no load on suspicious region until medical clearance.
5. **SCOPE medical:** Osteoporosis diagnosis, DXA, drug therapy, fracture care = physician; HP screens, stops, triages, fall-prevention within competence.
