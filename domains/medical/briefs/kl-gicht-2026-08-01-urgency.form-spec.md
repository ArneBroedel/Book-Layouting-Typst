## Learner context

- **Actor:** HP Physio / PT Direktzugang bei MSK / heißem Gelenk
- **Time pressure / scan budget:** 10–25 s for tier choice
- **Setting:** Praxis / Hausbesuch

## Learner job (one primary)

**choose** (+ **stop** on T1; **constrain** on SCOPE)

**Primary job in one sentence:** Assign the correct TIME tier for a hot mono / known-gout picture, execute that tier’s action, and never treat medical SCOPE (Punktion/IA/Kristall/Antibiose) as a quieter urgency step.

## Simple alternative (required)

- boring default: **4-row markdown-style table** (Situation | Dringlichkeit | Handlung) as in C  
- why not only that: under scan stress T1 (Sepsis-Lage) and T2 (septic-until-exclusion bulk) collapse into one “Notfall” blob; SCOPE becomes a fifth table row that reads like T5; stack + orthogonal frame reduces that class of error

## Load risks (if form is wrong)

- T1 visually equals T2 (both “hot joint red”)
- Empty loud 112 theater when case is T2 (known gout + fever without shock / afebrile non-loading mono)
- T3 looks like “may treat as gout” without medical framing gate
- SCOPE reads as green “may treat” clearance under T4
- Multi-trigger T2 becomes separate ladder rungs of urgency
- Solid-red full Master chrome (wrong genre)
- Absolute Kontraindikationen buried or omitted

## Cognitive / layout levers

- **Segmenting:** one block per TIME tier (all four present in C)
- **Signaling:** weight T1 ≫ T2 ≫ T3 ≫ T4 (size/fill/left bar + **label**, not color alone)
- **Contiguity:** triggers adjacent to Handlung inside same tier
- **Figure–ground:** SCOPE frame optically outside TIME stack
- **Multi-trigger:** T2 trigger groups inside **one** T2 card (dense compression)
- **Hard-stop chip:** absolute KI adjacent to T2/SCOPE, not a fifth tier

## Information architecture (zones)

| zone | loudness | content role | must feel like |
|---|---|---|---|
| TIME stack T1→T4 | T1 loudest | urgency + action payload | ordered priority landscape |
| T1 internal | max | one stop card + 2 trigger groups | **one** 112 card, not two rungs |
| T2 | loud | same-day medical path — **dense bulk** | distinct from T1; where most septic-until-exclusion lives |
| T3 | medium | already framed recurrent pattern → zeitnah FA/HA | gated — not free “Gicht-Pfad” |
| T4 | medium-quiet | treat + return net | permission with constraints |
| **SCOPE frame** | quiet-strong border | competence boundary | **not** a TIME tier (geometry); content title only |
| Absolute KI | quiet-strong | **action bans** until exclusion (not a second SCOPE frame) | chip/strip adjacent to T2/SCOPE; not T5; not duplicate SCOPE title |
| Quiet | quiet | sources later | not before action |

## Must-see elements

1. **T1 ≠ T2 ≠ T3** — separate labels and visual weight  
2. **T1 triggers** inside one card: (a) heißes Gelenk + Fieber/Systemik + AZ-Verschlechterung/Sepsis-Zeichen; (b) polyartikulärer Entzündungssturm mit schwerer Systembedrohung  
3. **T2 dense:** afebrile non-loading / strongest rest pain hot mono; fever/systemics even with known gout; post-IA/OP/Trauma; Hochrisiko; polyartikulärer Sturm without shock; Ulcus/Tophi + local infect signs — one card  
4. **T2 Handlung:** stop PT · same-day NA · septic until medical exclusion · handover pins  
5. **T3 gate:** only already medical-framed recurrent pattern **without** current hot mono non-loading/strong rest pain and **without** systemics  
6. **T4 constraints:** medical-framed stable pattern; no forced load in acute hot attack; return net  
7. **SCOPE:** Kristallnachweis · Gelenkpunktion · Antibiose · harnsäuresenkende Therapie · endgültige septisch-vs-kristallin = ärztlich; PT screent/triagiert; no puncture/IA  
8. **Absolute KI:** no forced manip / invasive local at unclear hot joint; no IA by HP; no probe-treat with fever/systemics  
9. Genre: teal framework Master; solid danger only on T1

## Urgency / weight encoding

- T1: solid danger fill + bold **112** label  
- T2: amber/warning weight — loud but **not** solid-danger twin of T1  
- T3: cool/info — delayed prioritized MD path  
- T4: teal/primary — constrained permission  
- SCOPE: border frame, no ladder fill progression that reads as “step 5 go”

## Branching / loop invariants

- Exactly four TIME tiers; SCOPE outside  
- No invented fifth urgency tier  
- T2 multi-triggers share one Handlung class (same-day medical)

## Genre chrome

- **Must look like:** kl-kurz Master TIME stack (teal framework)  
- **Must not look like:** solid-red Notfallkarte wall; traffic-light clearance; ontology form-meta captions
