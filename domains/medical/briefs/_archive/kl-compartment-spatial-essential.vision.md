# Graphic Vision Brief — Kompartment ESSENTIAL (no cut, no atlas labels)

- **slug:** `kl-compartment-spatial-essential`
- **generator:** gemini-web-nanobanana
- **structure_reference:** **none**

## One job

Show the **pathophysiological principle** of acute compartment syndrome:  
**rising pressure inside a closed, unyielding loge** → risk of tissue ischemia — not “simple sore muscle”.

## What NOT to do (hard)

- **No** axial / transverse **cross-section** (no MRI/CT-slice look — that implies high precision)
- **No** labeled bones (no Tibia / Fibula callouts)
- **No** four named compartments as an atlas inventory
- **No** multi-panel poster, no faces, no extra feet, no ambulance
- **No** English, no mmHg, no dense anatomy

## What TO do

**Option A (preferred):** Pure **abstract principle**  
- One closed ring / shell = unyielding boundary  
- Inner mass + arrows = rising pressure  
- Flat comic style, thick lines  

**Option B:** Very coarse **outer** lower-leg silhouette (side or front), **not a cut**, with one **tense red zone** under the skin at the front of the shin — clearly cartoon, not realistic anatomy.

## German text only (max 3 short labels total)

Pick only what is needed, e.g.:

- geschlossene Loge  
- Druck ↑  
- optional footer: Erhaltene Pulse schließen ACS nicht aus  

Or even fewer. **No structure names that invite “wrong anatomy” critique.**

## Style

First glance: **obviously schematic / comic**.  
Never: “precise medical imaging anatomy”.

## Success

5 seconds: “Pressure is trapped in a hard shell — dangerous, not muscle soreness.”  
Zero impulse to fact-check osteology.
