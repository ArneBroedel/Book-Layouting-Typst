# Graphic Vision Brief — Kompartment SPATIAL FOCUS (one idea only)

- **slug:** `kl-compartment-spatial-focus`
- **parent:** `kl-compartment-spatial.form-spec.md`
- **generator:** gemini-web-nanobanana
- **structure_reference:** **none**
- **ambition:** single-panel, simple, high fidelity

## One job only

Show **acute pressure in the anterior compartment of the lower leg** so the learner sees *why* “Muskelkater” is wrong: a **tense osteofascial loge** under the fascia, not a soft muscle ache.

**Do not** invent a multi-panel poster. **Do not** illustrate the full urgency matrix, autoinjector, or 5 different vignettes.

## Composition (strict)

- **Exactly one scene:** mid lower leg, either  
  (A) clean **axial cross-section** (preferred) with Tibia (large, medial) + Fibula (small, lateral) and **four compartments**, **anterior** filled/glowing tense; **or**  
  (B) lateral/oblique leg with **anterior compartment** clearly swollen/tense under skin (no extra limbs).
- White or light textbook background, medical illustration style (like anatomy atlas), teal/danger accents sparingly.
- **No second floating foot, no extra legs, no random body parts.**
- **No face / head / headache** (disproportional pain is *in the loge*, not a migraine face).
- **No** multi-column dashboard, no ambulance collage, no icon grid of 8 actions.

## Allowed German text only (minimal — at most 4 short labels)

| label | where |
|---|---|
| Vorderes Kompartiment | on/near the tense anterior loge |
| Tibia | on large bone |
| Fibula | on small bone |
| Brettharte Loge | short callout on tense anterior surface (optional) |

Optional one short footer line (single line, not a panel):  
`Erhaltene Pulse schließen ACS nicht aus`

**Forbidden text:** English “Warning”, mmHg numbers, invented doses, “EMERGENCY CARD”, long bullet essays, “Rettungsstelle”.

## Icon / anatomy meaning

- Anterior compartment = **firm, tense**, not soft pillow.  
- Bones anatomically plausible (tibia ≫ fibula).  
- If cross-section: compartments fill the oval; no hexagon doodle.

## Success criterion

Under 5-second scan: “pressure is trapped in the **front** lower-leg compartment — not a simple sore muscle.”
