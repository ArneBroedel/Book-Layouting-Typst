## Learner context

- **Actor:** HP Physio / PT Direktzugang under MSK-camouflage + patient/self bagatellization
- **Scan budget:** 8–20 s for false-frame rejection
- **Setting:** Praxis — patient cancels, jokes, „wants rest“, or suddenly seems calm after crisis

## Learner job (one primary)

**distinguish** (error frames)

**Primary job:** Hear the false everyday/MSK frame, map to real self-harm risk, **ask / protect / hand off** — do not continue „wie gewohnt“.

## Load risks (if form is wrong)

- Frames listed without danger side (quotes only)
- Meta-UI words („Label“, „Darunter“, „false belief“) instead of content role
- Softening „plötzliche Gelassenheit“ into recovery cue
- NSSV framed as harmless because no death wish
- Equal cell in a 2×2 with for/against (ontology: three sequential relations — mimic is **own** unit)
- Anatomy/pain body map as main figure (teaches MSK, hides safety job)
- Sensational illustration (dark figure, methods, stigma portrait) as „drama peel“
- Consequence text that claims PT psychiatric diagnosis

## Must-see elements

1. **Direction frame → danger** (not reverse; not overtriage satire)  
2. All **four canonical C frames** present  
3. **„Endlich ruhiger geworden“ / plötzliche Gelassenheit = Alarm**, not reassurance  
4. **„Das war nur ein Scherz“** = bagatellisierte Todeswünsche = Warnsignal  
5. **„Non-Compliance“** can mask severe depression + suicide thoughts  
6. No PT diagnosis claim; consequence = ask / do not isolate / assign path  
7. No methods/weapons/noose decorative art
