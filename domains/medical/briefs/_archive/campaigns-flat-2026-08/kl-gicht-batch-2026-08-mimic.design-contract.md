## Learner job (one primary)

**distinguish** (false-frame error class)

**Primary job in one sentence:** Reject three comforting labels that demote hot mono risk — including afebrile false security — and keep septic live.

## Form intensity (required)

- **Chosen level:** **L4** (schematic dual-band rows encoding frame→danger relation)
- **Why not L−1 (L3 callouts):** three separate callouts re-stack as prose; dual-band geometry forces the danger half into the same scan line as the frame.
- **Why not L+1 (L5 peel):** three short pairs do not need peel animation.

## Simple alternative (required)

## Form choice

- **Pattern name:** three-row dual-band frame→danger map  
- **Intensity level:** L4  
- **Medical-pedagogy reason:** Batch C’s distinctive false frames are **Distorsion/Bänder**, **known-gout-no-infect**, **no-fever-safe-gout** — each has a different danger payload; a single generic “don’t miss septic” banner loses the afebrile-security lesson. Row 3 differs from prior pilot’s knee-CPPD frame — must re-pin.
