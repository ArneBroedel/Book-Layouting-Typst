## Learner job (one primary)

**distinguish** (error class: false frame)

**Primary job in one sentence:** Reject three false explanations that demote a hot mono picture, and keep infection (and septic doubt even without fever) in play.

## Simple alternative (required)

- boring default: three prose bullets as in C  
- why not only that: under scan stress the **danger half** of each frame is buried after comforting frame language

## Load risks (if form is wrong)

- Only frame column visible  
- One generic “don’t miss septic” row instead of three distinct errors  
- Old pilot row „Knie=immer Gicht“ instead of batch C „Kein Fieber, also sicher Gicht“  
- English stage titles / form-meta

## Must-see elements

1. Exactly **three** rows matching C frames  
2. Each row: **frame phrase** + **why false / what remains**  
3. Row 1: „Umgeknickt — sicher Bänder“ → Podagra oft ohne adäquates Trauma; Distorsion-Fehldeutung  
4. Row 2: „Bekannte Gicht, also kein Infekt“ → vorbekannte Gicht schließt septische Arthritis nicht aus; Fieber/Systemik → Infektpfad  
5. Row 3: „Kein Fieber, also sicher Gicht“ → Fieberfreiheit entlastet septischen Zweifel nicht zuverlässig; heißes Gelenk + AZ-Verschlechterung → ärztlich klären  
6. No fourth invented frame; no old knee-CPPD row as substitute
