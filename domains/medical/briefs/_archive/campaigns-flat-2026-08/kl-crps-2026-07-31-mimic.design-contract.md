## Learner job (one primary)

**distinguish** (error mode)

**Primary job:** Refuse four false everyday frames that either bagatellize CRPS/emergencies or mislabel infection as “inflammatory CRPS”.

## Form intensity

- **Chosen level:** **L4** (row map frame→danger)
- **Why not L−1 (L3 callouts):** four parallel error mappings need equal row structure and directed relation; stacked callouts lose direction and equality under scan.
- **Why not L+1 (L5 peel diagram):** peel adds mask theater; four short C frames teach better as dense dual-band rows unless free vision later proves denser (optional only).

## Simple alternative

- [x] numbered / bulleted list (C prose)  
- [ ] plain prose  
- [ ] 2-column table without direction encoding  
- [ ] library pin-swap without chrome  

**Why not only floor:** list lacks **directed** frame→danger dual coding; learners remember slogans not the refusal rule.

## Space budget

- **Teaching gain vs next-lower intensity:** **clear**
- **Max vertical budget:** ☑ ~¼ page (hard cap ~⅓)
- **Why earned:** four frames with consequences; denser than ⅛ without pin truncation
- **If gain ≤ weak → step down:** if rows become sparse → compress type, do not expand page

## Form choice

- **Pattern name:** four-row frame→danger mimic map
- **Intensity level:** L4
- **Media-design reason:** Contiguity frame|arrow|danger; equal row height; muted vs emphasis dual coding; no meta UI words.
- **Medical-pedagogy reason:** CRPS label has **dual danger** (under- and over-label). Especially Fieber/Eiter-Vorrang and Psychosomatik-Entwarnung are high-error frames in Direktzugang Nachsorge.
