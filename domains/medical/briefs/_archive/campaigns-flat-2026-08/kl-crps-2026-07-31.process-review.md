## Failure modes (rank-ordered)

### F1 — First-glance pretty ≠ textbook-ready

Raster and polish can look “designed” while failing clinical/laterality/leader honesty under Human scrutiny.  
**Agent bias:** stop at “looks good on PNG thumbnail”.

### F2 — Free vision overloaded + labels-in-raster

One image tried to teach multi-pin dashboard (pain, allodynia, vegetative, trophisch, distal, side-compare, safety).  
Models then invent/garble labels, duplicate chips, and put leaders on arbitrary anatomy.

### F3 — Illustration-semantics incomplete

Audits checked text strings more than:

- laterality / side-compare *role truth*  
- leader → region meaning  
- same-hand palm/dorsum vs contralateral compare  

### F4 — Bad demotion ladder

When free FAIL, agent substituted **worse** “abstract” chrome (colored blocks, oval “hands”) instead of stepping down to **simple-alternative doctrine** (clinical table / list that already teaches).

### F5 — Density misread as page-count minimize

Space budget was twisted into: fewer pages + smaller type + tighter stacks = “done”.  
Human: empty sprawl is bad; **squished rhythm is also bad**.

### F6 — Process recovery without re-critique

After demote-to-blob, no fresh VC “worse-than-simple / decorative theater” pass before showing Human.

## Principles (flexible norms)

Use as **defaults with override reasons**, not absolute bans.

1. **Pretty-gate:** Do not Accept raster/hybrid on “looks polished”; require illustration-semantics + expert-plausible laterality/structure.  
2. **Focus free vision:** Prefer **one visual claim per gen**; default **no text/labels in free raster** — Typst owns German pins.  
3. **Anatomy/body free vision remains paused** unless H-Gfx; contralateral clinical compare is still a body graphic risk class.  
4. **Demote path = step down intensity:** FAIL free → L3–L4 clinical typography / table / map-rows — **never** decorative placeholder geometry.  
5. **Density is two-sided:** block sparse-sprawl *and* **squish-compress** (unreadable stacks, crushed margins for page-count vanity).  
6. **Solid unimpressive > pretty wrong:** Human “solid, workable” beats agent “ambitious FAIL”.  
7. **Page count is not a success metric;** teaching hierarchy + breathing room + pin honesty are.
