## Learner / didactic intent (from Form Spec)

- **unit type:** recognition (criteria cluster)
- **learner job:** run structured look ABCDE + Ugly Duckling; suspekt → assign, not destroy
- **load risk:** glossary table; EN letters only; invented mm; decorative moles without readable criteria
- **presentation hypothesis:** free spatial / mnemonic form for A–E + distinct Ugly Duckling beat
- **success criterion:** Under undressing stress learner recalls ABCDE + Ugly Duckling as checklist form and treats positive look as medical assignment
- **why this must look like a graphic:** the structured *look* must feel like a visual instrument, not a five-row glossary dump

## Must-see

1. All five letters **A B C D E** each with **German clinical meaning** next to / on the letter  
2. D: **Durchmesser typisch > 5 mm** (exactly this threshold wording — not 6 mm)  
3. E: **Evolution**  
4. **Ugly Duckling**: *eine Läsion unterscheidet sich klar von den übrigen Nävi*  
5. Micro action: bei Suspekt → **ärztliche Zuweisung**; **keine** Biopsie, Kürettage, destruktiven Maßnahmen  
6. German only — no English stage titles (“Action”, “Trigger cluster”, “Color variety” alone without DE)  
7. No form-meta / ontology IDs  
8. Not a full TIME/urgency ladder (that is a different unit)
