## Learner context

- **Actor:** PT Direktzugang under mislabel risk in post-trauma care
- **Scan budget:** 15–40 s
- **Setting:** Praxis / “Nachbehandlung”

## Learner job (one primary)

**distinguish** (error mode)

**Primary job:** See which everyday frames **mask** either emergency DDx or real CRPS risk — and refuse the false reassurance.

## Simple alternative

- boring default: numbered prose list of four frames (as C)  
- why not only that: direction frame→danger and dual-danger nature (over- and under-label) disappear in linear prose under scan

## Load risks (if form is wrong)

- Meta-UI words („Label“, „Darunter“, „FRAME“)  
- Reverse direction (danger → joke)  
- Equal cells in a 2×2 with for/against  
- Only one danger direction (forget Fieber/Eiter-Vorrang **or** forget bagatellisierte CRPS)  
- Comic peel theater without denser teaching  

## Cognitive / layout levers

- Contiguity: frame next to danger consequence  
- Signaling: danger louder than frame  
- Dual coding: directed mark + short consequence  
- Segmenting: four equal rows  

## Information architecture (zones)

| zone | loudness | content role | must feel like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | medium | Trügerische Erklärungen | error-mode title (content words only) |
| Mapping rows (4) | medium-loud | frame → danger/consequence | directed map |
| Quiet | quiet | optional link to T1/T3 | not competing |

## Must-see elements

1. Direction **frame → danger** (not reverse)  
2. All **four** C frames  
3. Row for **Fieber/Eiter → Infekt-Vorrang vor CRPS-Label**  
4. Row that blocks **Psychosomatik-Entwarnung** for organische Notfall-DDx **and** CRPS  
5. No print words „Label“ / „Darunter“ as UI chrome  
6. No form-meta  

## Icon meaning

| pin / zone | drawing must show | must not show |
|---|---|---|
| frame side | muted quoted everyday phrase | smiling “OK” clearance |
| danger side | weight/emphasis on real risk | gore / anatomy |

## Urgency / weight encoding

- Frame: neutral/muted, often Anführungszeichen  
- Danger: danger or strong emphasis + bold  
- Arrow/relation: danger-accent  

## Branching / loop invariants

- Exactly four rows from C — do not invent fifth frame  
- Do not merge with FA/NM into one 2×2  

## Genre chrome

- **Must look like:** Master illness-script error-mode map  
- **Must not look like:** entertainment comic strip; red emergency wall

## Explicit non-goals / must-not

- No invented fifth trap  
- No English “mimic” stage title  
- No reverse satire (overtriage comedy)  
- No claim rewrite  

## Presentation hypothesis (pattern name)

four-row frame→danger mimic map (dual-direction CRPS label risk)

## Success criterion

Under scan stress the learner **refuses all four false frames** and especially **does not keep treating under Fieber/Eiter as “inflammatory CRPS”**.
