## Learner context

- **Actor:** HP Physio first contact / Nachbehandlung post-Trauma
- **Scan budget:** 10–20 s gestalt + 30 s pin check
- **Setting:** Praxis — hand or foot after fracture/OP

## Learner job (one primary)

**recognize**

**Primary job in one sentence:** See the **distal regional** pattern (disproportional pain + allodynia + vegetative/trophic asymmetry) as a CRPS *signal* that still requires emergency DDx exclusion — without reading a full table.

## Simple alternative

- boring default: the §3 4-domain table only  
- why not only that: table teaches channels but **under-teaches the co-located distal regional gestalt** (asymmetry, shine, touch intolerance) that makes CRPS recognizable in seconds; free vision/hybrid cluster dual-codes that gestalt

## Load risks (if form is wrong)

- Looks like anatomy atlas / osteofascial dissection  
- Looks like definitive CRPS diagnosis poster (overclaim)  
- Icons contradict pins (e.g. “warm” icon for pulse-loss emergency)  
- Sparse full-page hero with three words  
- English labels / form-meta  
- Patient-identifiable photography aesthetic as clinical claim  
- Collapses into generic “pain limb” stock without regional distal emphasis  

## Cognitive / layout levers

- Dual coding: small schematic limb/hand **plus** pin chips  
- Contiguity: side-compare cue next to color/temp asymmetry  
- Segmenting: 4–6 must-see cue chips, not 20  
- Signaling: Allodynie + Disproportionalität + distal regional loudest  
- Quiet strip: “kein Ersatz für Notfall-DDx / Budapest ärztlich” without form-meta ontology jargon

| zone | loudness | content role | must feel like |

## Must-see elements

1. **Distales, regionales** Verteilungsmuster (distal of trauma site — not whole-body)  
2. **Disproportionaler** Schmerz (stärker als zur Verletzung passend)  
3. **Allodynie** / Berührungsempfindlichkeit  
4. **Vegetative/trophische** Zeichen from C: Temperatur- und/oder Farbdifferenz, Schwellung, Schwitzen, trophische Veränderungen (Haut, Nägel, Behaarung) — **not** elevating Fall-only “Haut glänzt” to a formal must-see criterion  
5. **Seitenvergleich** cue  
6. Safety brake using **C role pins only** (Budapest ärztlich; Notfall-Red-Flags / DDx first) — **not** a green clearance or novel slogan law  

## Style constraint (free vision)

- **Prefer:** schematic / flat didactic medical illustration

## Icon meaning (free vision draws criteria)

| pin / zone | drawing must show | must not show |
|---|---|---|
| Disproportionalität | pain weight **out of proportion** to a small injury mark / cast context | numeric pain scale invention |
| Allodynie | light touch → strong pain reaction | random lightning gore |
| Temp/Farbe | clear **asymmetry** left vs right (warm/red or cool/livide variants OK if labeled from C options) | both sides identical |
| Schwellung | asymmetric soft-tissue fullness distal | elephantiasis comedy |
| Trophik | altered skin / nails / hair trophic hint (from §3 Beobachtung) | unrelated rash atlas; **do not require** standalone “glänzt” criterion label |
| Distal regional | emphasis **distal** segment of one extremity | whole-body dermatome map |
| Side-compare | two sides visible or explicit L/R compare mark | single orphan limb with no compare |
| Safety brake | quiet C-faithful roles only (see claim pins) | form-meta; invented slogan not in C |
