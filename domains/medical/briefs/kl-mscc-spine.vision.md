## Learner / didactic intent

- **unit type:** recognition + danger support
- **learner job:** recognize spine metastasis / MSCC stop pattern (night pain + neuro + 112)
- **load risk:** pretty spine art without claims; invented Kraftgrad or score thresholds; form-meta captions
- **hypothesis:** spatial spine map, labels on referents (contiguity)
- **success criterion:** see spine + night/progredient pain + Parese/Gang/Blase as one stop pattern; 112/NA; no MSK continue

## Must-see

1. Spine localization (Wirbelkörper / thorakolumbal / epidural cue)
2. Red-flag pain pattern: nächtlich / ruhebetont / progredient / weckt
3. Neurological / MSCC cues: neue motorische Schwäche (jede), Gangstörung, Blasen-/Mastdarmstörung
4. German pin labels only
5. **112** / Notaufnahme cue short — not full protocol essay
6. Optional short KI: keine Belastung · keine HVT
