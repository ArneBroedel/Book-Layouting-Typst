## Overall presentation goal

Learner in Direktzugang **sees CRPS as a regional post-trauma pattern that must never outrank emergency DDx**, **scans four TIME tiers correctly** (112 for vascular/compartment/systemic infection; same-day for progressive systemic-without-112; **zeitnah Facharzt** for true CRPS-Arbeitsverdacht; treat+net only with specialist co-care), **reads the distal vegetative/trophic + allodynia gestalt as a recognition signal**, **rejects the four false frames**, and **keeps Budapest/Systemtherapie outside PT competence**.

Layout must make **dual danger of the label** (mask emergencies *and* bagatellize real CRPS) and **T1≠T3** self-evident without form-meta captions.
