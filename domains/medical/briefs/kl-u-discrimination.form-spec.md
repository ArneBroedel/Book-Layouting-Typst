## Information architecture (zones)

| zone | loudness | content role | must feel like |
|---|---|---|---|
| For / Against | medium | weight H | two poles, same job family |
| Must-not-miss | medium-loud | parallel dangers | own strip, not a fourth equal cell |
| (later sibling) Mimic | medium-loud | frame error | separate unit (already accepted M-A) |

## Must-see elements

1. Two columns or stacks: **Erhärtet** \| **Passt eher nicht** (content words only)  
2. **Nicht übersehen** as separate louder strip below — not in a 2×2 grid  
3. Erysipel pins copy-through  
4. No meta form labels (no „HYP-H“, no „Relation 2“)  
5. Must-not-miss items scannable as list  

## Urgency / weight encoding

- For: teal/primary edge (support, not 112-red)  
- Against: muted  
- Must-not-miss: warning edge, not equal grey to Against  
- Never paint „Erhärtet“ as solid T1 danger  

## Genre chrome

- **Must look like:** clinical reasoning block in a learning script  
- **Must not look like:** emergency wall; equal four-quadrant poster
