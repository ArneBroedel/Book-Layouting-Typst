## Learner context

- **Actor:** PT Direktzugang under mislabel risk  
- **Scan budget:** 15–40 s  
- **Setting:** Praxis  

## Learner job (one primary)

**distinguish** (error mode)

**Primary job:** See which everyday/MSK frame **masks** which real danger and why delay hurts.

## Load risks (if form is wrong)

- Meta-UI words („Label“, „Darunter“)  
- Reverse direction (overtriage satire)  
- Equal cell in a 2×2 with for/against  
- Prose wall without mapping  

## Cognitive / layout levers

- Contiguity: frame next to danger  
- Signaling: danger louder than frame  
- Dual coding: structure (arrow) + short consequence  
- No wireframe chrome in print  

## Information architecture (zones)

| zone | loudness | content role | must feel like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | medium | „Trügerisch — Gefahr hinter harmlosem Bild“ | error-mode title |
| Mapping rows | medium-loud | each frame → danger (+ consequence) | directed map |
| Quiet | quiet | link to T1/T2 if needed | not competing |

## Must-see elements

1. Direction **frame → danger** (not reverse)  
2. At least Erysipel four maps (pins below)  
3. No print words „Label“ / „Darunter“ as UI  
4. Consequence of delay visible on ≥1 rows (Therapieverzögerung)  

## Urgency / weight encoding

- Frame: neutral/muted, often in Anführungszeichen  
- Danger: danger color + bold  
- Arrow/relation mark: danger-accent  
- Not a TIME ladder  

## Genre chrome

- **Must look like:** clinical error-mode map inside learning script  
- **Must not look like:** comic without claims; anatomy shock art; wireframe mock
