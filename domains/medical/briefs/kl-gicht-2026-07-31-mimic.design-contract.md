## Form choice

- **Pattern name:** three-row dual-band frame→danger map  
- **Intensity level:** L4  
- **Media-design reason:** Segmenting one error class per row; dual coding frame vs danger by fill/weight; contiguity of pair; dense type.  
- **Medical-pedagogy reason:** Gicht’s distinctive false frames are **Distorsion**, **known-gout-only**, **knee-always-gout** — each has a different danger payload; a single generic “don’t miss septic” banner loses the crystal-doesn’t-clear and CPPD lessons.
