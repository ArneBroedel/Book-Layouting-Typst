## Scene (free vision prompt essence)

Didactic medical illustration for a textbook page (not a photo, not gore):

- **Subject:** schematic pair of human hands (or distal forearms + hands), top-down or slight 3/4, clean white/cream clinical background.
- **Asymmetry:** one hand looks post-trauma regional CRPS *signal* — slightly swollen, warmer/redder or livider skin tone, shinier/trophic-looking skin, protective posture; the other hand is the normal comparison side.
- **Cues as clear labeled chips or callouts in German** (not English):  
  - Disproportionaler Schmerz  
  - Allodynie (leichte Berührung)  
  - Temperatur-/Farbdifferenz  
  - Asymmetrische Schwellung  
  - Trophische Veränderungen (Haut, Nägel)  
  - Distales regionales Muster  
  - Seitenvergleich  
- **Optional quiet strip** in German only: *Budapest-Diagnose und Systemtherapie erfolgen ärztlich* · *HP Physio screent und triagiert*
- **Style:** flat schematic medical textbook illustration, vector-like, teal/amber/neutral clinical palette, high label legibility, dense composition (~⅓ page worth of information, not sparse hero art).
- **Must NOT:** photoreal patient photo; blood/open wounds; full anatomy dissection; English words; “CRPS diagnosis confirmed” stamp; solid red emergency poster; whole-body map; form-meta words (TIME, SCOPE, ontology IDs); empty icon wells; large empty whitespace.
