**Why not simple (WP9b/c):**  
Pure bullets bury hierarchy. Need dual coding frame quiet / danger loud + row-4 Infekt alert — **without** multi-band peel cards that burn a full page for four claims.

## Space budget

- **Teaching gain vs compact text/list:** clear  
- **Max vertical budget:** ~⅓ page (WP9c)  
- **Why earned:** dual-band map rows (mask left / truth right) teach error hierarchy denser than peel stacks  
- **WP9b failure:** four tall peel cards + edge strips = **sparse-sprawl**  

## Form intensity

- **Chosen level:** **L4** (schematic dual-band rows)  
- **Why not L−1 (L2–L3 only):** pure emphasis text still equalizes frame and danger under scan; need spatial dual coding  
- **Why not L+1 (L5 peel / L6 raster SoT):** density; pins fit L4; free vision provenance only  

## Form choice

- **Pattern name:** dense dual-band map rows (frame | danger fill) ×4 + row-4 alert  
- **Intensity level:** L4  
- **Media-design reason:** Contiguity + loudness dual coding in **one row height** per trap; no third “edge” band; tight stack spacing.  
- **Medical-pedagogy reason:** Same four frames/dangers; Infekt priority via border/fill only — no vertical theater.
