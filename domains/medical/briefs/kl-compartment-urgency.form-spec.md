## Learner context

- **Actor:** HP Physio / PT Direktzugang  
- **Time pressure / scan budget:** 5–20 s tier + action  
- **Setting:** Praxis / post-trauma / post-OP / cast-bandage patient  

## Learner job (one primary)

**choose** (+ **stop** on T1/T2) · secondary **constrain** (SCOPE)

**Primary job in one sentence:** Assign 112 vs Notaufnahme/Unfallchirurgie vs treat-with-net correctly, execute the stop payload (including **no elevation above heart**), and never treat SCOPE as a quieter urgency step.

## Load risks (if form is wrong)

- T1 and T2 collapse into one „Notfall“ tile  
- SCOPE as grey T5 / green bottom light  
- Invented T3  
- T2 missing anti-elevation or “also with pulses”  
- CECS/T4 as free-pass without Re-Triage  
- PT techniques continue (load, compression, forced stretch, deep myofascial)  
- Solid-red full Master wall (III chrome)  
- Form-meta captions  

## Cognitive / layout levers

- **Segmenting:** one block per TIME tier present (T1, T2, T4 only)  
- **Signaling:** T1 ≫ T2 ≫ T4 with **labels**, not color alone  
- **Contiguity:** triggers next to action inside tier  
- **Figure–ground:** SCOPE outside stack  
- **Collapse:** no empty T3 slot

## Must-see elements

1. **T1 ≠ T2** — separate labels and visual weight  
2. T1 triggers: rasche Verschlechterung; progrediente Neuro (**Parese**, **Hypästhesie**); schwere AZ-Verschlechterung  
3. T2 triggers: disproportionaler Schmerz und/oder passiver Dehnungsschmerz und/oder brettharte Loge — **auch bei tastbaren Pulsen**; Schmerz unter engem Gips/Verband  
4. T2 action includes: **sofort abbrechen**; neither Belastung nor Kompression, forcierte Dehnung, tiefe myofasziale Releases; Verband **lockern/öffnen**; Extremität **nicht über Herzhöhe lagern**; unverzüglich NA/Unfallchirurgie  
5. T4 includes Re-Triage on escalation  
6. **SCOPE separate geometry** — Druckmessung, Fasziotomie-Indikation, OP-Entlastung, endgültige DDx art. Ischämie/nekrot. Infektion = ärztlich  
7. **No T3**  
8. Genre: script/teal; solid danger only on T1

## Urgency / weight encoding

- T1: solid danger + bold **112**  
- T2: warning/amber + **Notaufnahme / Unfallchirurgie**  
- T4: primary/teal + **Behandeln mit Sicherheitsnetz**  
- SCOPE: square frame / hairline; content title only  
- Multi-triggers **inside** a tier collapse to that tier (no fake ladder from symptom list)

## Branching / loop invariants

- No inventing T3  
- No collapsing T1/T2  
- T4 never without Re-Triage / net  
- Posttrauma/-OP treat only after **ärztlicher Freigabe** (SCOPE constrains T4)  
- ACS is **not** a PT treatment case
