## Didactic goal (one job)

Pathophysiological principle of **acute compartment syndrome** at the **lower leg**:  
pressure rises inside a **closed osteofascial loge** (especially **anterior**), risk of ischemia — not “simple muscle soreness”.

## Visual hierarchy (critical)

**First glance must say: “this is a schematic / didactic diagram.”**  
Second glance may reward: the few structures that *are* shown are **topologically correct**.

**Never** the reverse: first glance “high-detail precise anatomy atlas”, then many missing/wrong structures.

## Style (mandatory)

- Flat **medical comic / textbook schematic** (like simplified teaching cards)
- Thick outlines, few flat color fills, no photoreal skin texture, no bone micro-detail (no trabeculae, no photoreal muscle fibers)
- Light background, calm teal/grey + **one** danger accent on the anterior loge
- Single panel only — **no** multi-vignette poster, no faces, no ambulance, no extra limbs

## Anatomy that MUST be present (and correct topology)

Axial **mid lower-leg cross-section** (schematic oval of soft tissue):

| Structure | How to draw (simple, correct) |
|-----------|--------------------------------|
| **Tibia** | Large bone cross-section, **anteromedial** (bigger) |
| **Fibula** | Small bone cross-section, **lateral** (much smaller) |
| **Anterior compartment** | Between tibia and fibula on the **anterior** side — **highlighted** (warm/red fill, “tense”) |
| **Lateral compartment** | Lateral to fibula — quiet fill |
| **Deep posterior** | Posterior to interosseous region — quiet fill |
| **Superficial posterior** | Largest posterior mass — quiet fill |
| **Interosseous membrane** (optional thin line) | Between tibia and fibula only if simple |

Do **not** label every muscle by name. Do **not** invent vessel trees in high detail.

## Patho principle (must read in 3 seconds)

- Anterior loge looks **tense / pressurized** vs quieter other compartments  
- Optional short arrows **inside** anterior compartment only (= pressure)  
- Outer boundary of the loge reads as **unyielding** (simple ring line)

## German labels only (max 6 short)

| Label | Placement |
|-------|-----------|
| Vorderes Kompartiment | in/on the highlighted anterior zone |
| Tibia | near large bone |
| Fibula | near small bone |
| Brettharte Loge | callout on anterior surface (optional) |
| Druck ↑ | inside anterior zone (optional) |
| Erhaltene Pulse schließen ACS nicht aus | single footer line (optional) |

**Forbidden text:** English, mmHg numbers, long essays, “EMERGENCY CARD”, inventing extra diagnoses.

## Success criterion

Under scan stress: “Anterior lower-leg compartment is a **closed pressure chamber** — schematic, but the bones/compartments are in the right places.”
