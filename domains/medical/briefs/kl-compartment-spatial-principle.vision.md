# Graphic Vision Brief — Kompartment PRINCIPLE (schematic only)

- **slug:** `kl-compartment-spatial-principle`
- **generator:** gemini-web-nanobanana **or** Typst code preferred
- **structure_reference:** **none**

## One job

Show the **pathological principle**: pressure rising **inside a closed, unyielding loge** → ischemia risk.  
**Not** a correct anatomical atlas of the lower leg.

## Style (mandatory)

- **Coarse, flat, abstract** medical schematic  
- Simple shapes: ring = boundary, core = pressure, arrows = rising pressure  
- Light background, few colors  
- **Must look schematic** — nobody should treat it as reference anatomy  

## Forbidden

- Photoreal skin, realistic bones with cortex/trabeculae  
- Multi-panel posters, faces, extra feet, ambulances  
- mmHg numbers, English chrome  
- Any cut that pretends to be a complete cross-section of all correct structures  

## Allowed German labels (≤5)

- geschlossene Loge  
- Druck ↑  
- Brettharte Loge (optional)  
- Erhaltene Pulse schließen ACS nicht aus (optional footer)  
- PT abbrechen / nicht über Herzhöhe (optional micro action)

## Success

5 s: “Druck steckt in einer festen Hülle — nicht harmloser Muskelkater.”
