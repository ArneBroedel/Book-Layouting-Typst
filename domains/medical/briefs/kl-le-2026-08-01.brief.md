## Overall presentation goal

Learner in Direktzugang **stops probe-treating on any plausible LE**, **scans nested multi-T1 112 triggers as one stop card** (not four ladder rungs), **separates stable-suspect T2 (absolute Schonung, NA/gleichtags) from T4 only after secure MSK or medical clearance**, **never treats Wells/YEARS as exclusion**, and **rejects the four false frames** — especially palpation-positive BWS when Dyspnoe/Tachykardie/VTE context co-travel.

Layout must make **T1 ≠ T2**, **Wells in SCOPE not TIME**, and **no T3** self-evident without form-meta captions.
