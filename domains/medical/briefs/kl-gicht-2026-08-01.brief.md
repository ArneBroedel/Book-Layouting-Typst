## Overall presentation goal

Learner in Direktzugang **sees Gicht as a high-acute monoarthritis pattern that never clears septic arthritis**, **scans four TIME tiers correctly** (112 only for Sepsis-Lage / severe multi-joint storm; same-day for the bulk of hot mono / non-loading / fever-even-with-known-gout; zeitnah only for already framed recurrent pattern; treat+net only after medical framing), **reads the hot mono (esp. MTP-I) gestalt as recognition signal**, **rejects the three false frames**, and **keeps Punktion / IA / Kristall / Antibiose / harnsäuresenkend outside PT competence**.

Layout must make **„bekannte Gicht / Kristalle entwarnen nicht“** and **T1 ≠ T2** (Sepsis-Lage vs septic-until-exclusion same-day) self-evident without form-meta captions.
