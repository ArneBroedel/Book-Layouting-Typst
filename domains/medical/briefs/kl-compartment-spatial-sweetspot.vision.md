# Graphic Vision Brief — Kompartment SWEET SPOT (patho principle, textbook level)

- **slug:** `kl-compartment-spatial-sweetspot`
- **generator:** gemini-web-nanobanana
- **structure_reference:** **none**

## The idea (must be unmistakable)

Acute compartment syndrome is **not** “the whole calf swelling outward.”

It is:

1. **Muscle (and vessels/nerves)** sit inside a **fascial loge**.  
2. The **fascia is unyielding** (does not expand like soft skin).  
3. If content volume tries to rise (edema, bleeding), **outer expansion is blocked**.  
4. Therefore **pressure rises inside** the loge → threatens **perfusion and nerves**.

## First glance

Should read as a **clear didactic schematic / medical comic** (thick lines, flat color) — **not** as CT/MRI precision anatomy, **not** as a toy “red balloon on a leg.”

## Composition (one panel)

**Preferred metaphor (not MRI slice inventory):**

- A **rigid shell / fascial envelope** drawn as a firm closed boundary (thicker, cooler color).  
- **Inside:** simplified muscle mass (warm/reddish when tense).  
- **Inside (small):** simplified **vessel + nerve** symbols being **compressed** (not photoreal neurovascular atlas).  
- **Pressure:** arrows or force marks that show pressure **building against the inner side of the rigid shell** (inward stress / cannot expand) — **NOT** arrows flying outward into free space as if the calf inflates freely.  
- Optional tiny “X” or lock mark on the shell: **Volumen kann nicht ausweichen**.

Optional: very coarse lower-leg **outer silhouette** as context, with the loge drawn as an **inset principle** — still one idea.

## Labels (German only, few, patho not osteology)

Max ~5 short labels, e.g.:

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Faszie (unnachgiebig) | rigid envelope |
| Muskulatur | content |
| Gefäß / Nerv (optional) | threatened structures inside |
| Druck ↑ (innen) | internal pressure |
| Volumen kann nicht ausweichen | key mechanism |

Optional footer: `Erhaltene Pulse schließen ACS nicht aus`

**Do not** label Tibia, Fibula, four compartments as an atlas.  
**Do not** invent mmHg.

## Forbidden

- Free expansion arrows that make the leg look freely thicker  
- Whole-calf uniform red fill without a closed shell  
- Photoreal atlas cut / CT look  
- Multi-panel chaos, UI chrome, English  
- Empty “principle ring only” with no medical content  
- Over-detailed wrong anatomy

## Success criterion

In 5–10 s the learner sees: **closed fascial shell + content cannot expand → internal pressure rises → muscle/NV at risk** — at textbook didactic level, without faking complete anatomy.
