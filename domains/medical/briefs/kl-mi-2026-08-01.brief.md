## Overall presentation goal

Learner in Direktzugang **maps plausible ACS (classic or atypically masked: shoulder/BWS/epigastrium/dyspnea) to Sofort-112**, **stops load and manual thorax “workup”**, **rejects MSK/GI/panic frames and palpation-alone clearance**, and **keeps EKG / Troponin / STEMI–NSTEMI split / MHS-Ausschluss outside PT competence**. Layout must make **nested multi-T1** (four 112 clusters = one stop card), **T1 ≠ T2**, **no T3**, and **Default-to-Danger** self-evident without form-meta captions.
