## Learner context

- **Actor:** HP Physio first contact with painful toe / mono joint
- **Time pressure / scan budget:** 5–12 s gestalt recognition
- **Setting:** Praxis; patient may say “Podagra again”

## Learner job (one primary)

**recognize** (+ stop-awareness)

**Primary job in one sentence:** Instantly read a hot mono joint picture (classically MTP-I) as inflammatory monoarthritis gestalt that can be crystal **or** septic — never as cleared “only gout.”

## Simple alternative (required)

- boring default: **§3 Beobachtung/Untersuchung bullets** or a quiet two-line callout  
- why not only that: recognition job is **visual pattern** (Rötung/Wärme/Schwellung/Schonhaltung); pure text under-teaches the “looks like this” anchor that anchors triage later  
- if free vision FAILs density/claim: step to **L3–L4 solid clinical panel** with same pins — never decorative geometry theater

## Load risks (if form is wrong)

- Anatomy dissection / bone atlas distracts from surface inflammatory cues  
- Photoreal patient photo with clinical overclaim  
- Multi-pin labeled limb dashboard (too many leaders) as default ambition  
- Pretty toe illustration **without** septic-until-exclusion pairing → trains “this is gout”  
- Sparse full-page single claim  
- Laterality invent (C Fall is right toe; free vision may stay schematic without side claim)  
- Form-meta captions (“U-hot-joint”)  

## Cognitive / layout levers

- **Dual coding:** picture of hot mono + short safety pairing text  
- **Signaling:** inflammatory surface cues (redness, swelling, guarded posture) louder than internal anatomy  
- **Segmenting:** one focused site (MTP-I classic) not whole-body collage  
- **Figure–ground:** clinical foot/toe region vs quiet background  
- **Contiguity:** safety line next to or under gestalt, not on a distant page
