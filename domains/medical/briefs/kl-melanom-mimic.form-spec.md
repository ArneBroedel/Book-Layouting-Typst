## Learner context

- **Actor:** PT Direktzugang  
- **Scan budget:** 8–15 s for false-frame rejection  
- **Setting:** Praxis, patient bagatellizes lesion  

## Learner job

**distinguish** (error frames)

**Primary job:** Hear the false frame, map to real danger, stop local treatment / assign.

## Load risks

- Frames listed without danger side  
- “Ugly” UI labels (“false belief”) instead of content role  
- Softening subungual / back inspection rows

| zone | loudness | role | feel |

## Must-see

1. All four content frames present  
2. Arrow/map to real danger (not just quote the patient)  
3. Subungual trauma-frame and back-inspection frame included  
4. No diagnosis claim by PT in consequence text
