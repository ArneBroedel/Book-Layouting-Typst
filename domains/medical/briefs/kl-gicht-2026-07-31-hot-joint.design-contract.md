- **Teaching gain vs next-lower intensity:** **clear** (essential only if free vision actually delivers readable inflammatory mono gestalt)

- **Density gate:** if free vision is sparse hero or unreadable blob → demote; gain becomes weak at L6 → step down  
- **If gain ≤ weak → step down:** yes — mandatory  

## Form choice

- **Pattern name:** focused hot-mono / Podagra gestalt + septic-until-exclusion safety pairing  
- **Intensity level:** L6 (with L4 fallback)  
- **Media-design reason:** Dual coding picture+safety; focused figure–ground on MTP-I region; no multi-pin leader dashboard; dense book crop.  
- **Medical-pedagogy reason:** Danger line is the chapter’s stop-awareness core; learners mislabel hot mono as sprain or “only gout.” Gestalt anchors later TIME choices without absorbing the TIME stack.

## Reading order

1. Hot mono MTP-I picture (gestalt)  
2. Safety pairing (danger line meaning)  
3. Optional short cue chips only if hybrid needs them
