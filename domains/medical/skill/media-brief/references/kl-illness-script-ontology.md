## Primary axes

| Axis | Meaning | Forbidden |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **TIME** | How fast to act (T1–T4) | Collapsing T1 with T2; treating SCOPE as a tier |
| **SCOPE** | Competence boundary | Ladder step under Treat; grey “T5” |

**ACTION** = payload *inside* each TIME tier (triggers + Handlung) — **not** a third axis.

### TIME scale

| # | Label | Notes |
|---|--------|--------|
| T1 | 112 / Sofort | Rescue now |
| T2 | Notaufnahme / gleichtags / noch heute | Same-day medical |
| T3 | Zeitnah Arzt | **Optional** — only if content fills it |
| T4 | Behandeln + Sicherheitsnetz | No “Treat without net” |

**Multi-trigger:** many 112 rows → **one** T1 card with internal trigger list.

## Epistemic dimensions (not layout axes)

| Dim | Unit | Job |
|-----|------|-----|
| PROFILE | U-enabling | recognize |
| CAUSAL | U-mechanism | understand |
| CHANNEL | U-cues (4 domains) | recognize |
| HYP-H | U-for-against | distinguish |
| HYP-OTHER | U-must-not-miss | stop-awareness + distinguish |
| FRAME-ERROR | U-mimic | distinguish (error) |
| TRANSFER | U-transfer | apply |

**CERTAINTY** = property of SCOPE (score ≠ rule-out).  
**COMMUNICATION** = loudness modifier on qualifiers (not an axis).

### SCORE / CERTAINTY hygiene (portfolio 2026-08 — LE / Stroke / MI)

Clinical prediction or risk **scores** in C (examples: **Wells**, **YEARS**, **ABCD²**, **Marburger Herz-Score**, similar “Items stark positiv”) are **not** TIME tiers.

| Put them… | Never as… |
|---|---|
| Inside **SCOPE** / competence brake (“Sensibilisierung, kein HP-Ausschluss”) | A green/amber ladder step between T2 and T4 |
| As a **qualifier inside T2** text when C uses them as same-day triage cue | “Score low → may treat” clearance |
| As warnhinweis language only | Rule-out or final LE/ACS/Stroke exclusion |

Geometry still: SCOPE frame outside TIME stack. No form-meta caption “not a score tier.”

## Invariants (designer checklist)

1. T1 ≠ T2 visually and by label.  
2. ACTION stays inside TIME tiers.  
3. Multi-triggers stay inside one tier.  
4. SCOPE optically outside TIME (geometry) — **never** explain “not a urgency tier” *inside* the figure.  
5. No Treat-without-net.  
6. Epistemic units do not break TIME/SCOPE layout.  
7. U-ddx only as **delta** vs must-not-miss (else omit).  
8. §4 = three relations sequential — **not** one 2×2.  
9. §3 = four equal channels — **not** a fake matrix.  
10. Claim pins copy-through from Content C only.  
11. Scores (Wells/YEARS/ABCD²/MHS/…) = **CERTAINTY/SCOPE**, not TIME.  
12. **T3 collapse:** if C has no zeitnah tier → no empty T3 and no invented T3.

## Form-language rule (Human 2026-07-26)

> **No form-meta inside the graphic.**  
> Separation of TIME vs SCOPE (etc.) must be **self-evident from geometry**.  
> Allowed: content titles (`Grenze der eigenen Zuständigkeit`, `Trügerische Erklärungen`).  
> Forbidden: “keine Dringlichkeitsstufe”, “Trigger cluster”, ontology IDs, EN stage chrome.  
> Designer notes only outside the figure (spike footer, Decision Note).
