## Learner context

- **Actor:** PT im Direktzugang (MSK-Behandlung; Mitinspektion bei Entkleidung)
- **Time pressure / scan budget:** ~15–30 s structured look during undressing / local inspection
- **Setting:** Praxis (Rücken, Nacken und Behandlungsgebiet mitsehen — content context; not a full body map invent)

## Learner job (one primary)

**recognize**

**Primary job in one sentence:** Run a structured visual check (ABCDE + Ugly Duckling) and treat a suspekte / Ugly-Duckling-positive Läsion as reason for **ärztliche Zuweisung**, not as PT-Hautdiagnose or local destruction.

## Load risks (if form is wrong)

- Five equal table rows with no hierarchy → mnemonic fails under scan stress  
- Invented mm cut-offs or staging depths not in C  
- Decorative mole art without readable DE criteria  
- English-only letter expansions as primary labels  
- Absorbing full TIME ladder into this unit (urgency belongs to §5 Spec)  
- Form-meta captions inside the figure  
- Solid-red emergency chrome for a recognition checklist  

## Cognitive / layout levers (only what layout can fix)

- **Segmenting:** one letter zone per criterion, ordered A→E  
- **Contiguity:** letter glyph immediately next to DE meaning (no foot-only legend)  
- **Signaling:** letter spine heavier than trailing notes; Ugly Duckling visually distinct from letters  
- **Dual coding (ambition):** optional spatial lesion/field + short text pins — labels remain claim-true  
- **Genre weight:** teal/primary recognition chrome, not T1 red wall

## Must-see elements

What must be *visible* (not only somewhere in prose):

1. **All five letters** with **German clinical meanings from C** (see pins)  
2. **A** Asymmetrie  
3. **B** unscharfe/unregelmäßige Begrenzung  
4. **C** Farbvielfalt (Color)  
5. **D** Durchmesser **typisch > 5 mm** (only mm claim allowed from C)  
6. **E** Evolution  
7. **Ugly Duckling:** eine Läsion unterscheidet sich klar von den übrigen Nävi  
8. Job sense: strukturierter Blick → bei Suspekt **rechtzeitige ärztliche Zuweisung** (not Exzision by PT)  
9. No PT Melanom-Diagnose / no Biopsie-Kürettage call as learner action in this unit  
10. No form-meta / ontology IDs in the figure  

## Urgency / weight encoding

- Recognition chrome (framework teal / primary) — **not** solid-red emergency wall  
- Optional small pointer: bei ABCDE-suspekt / Ugly Duckling → **zuweisen** (gleichtags–zeitnah lives in §5 Spec, not re-laddered here)  
- Letter order is sequence weight, not TIME tiers  
- Ugly Duckling is **not** quieter than letters; it is a parallel second sign  

## Branching / loop invariants (if any)

- Exactly **five** letter criteria A–E; Ugly Duckling is **outside** the letter set  
- No sixth letter “U”; no invented F/G criteria  
- No diameter threshold other than **typisch > 5 mm**  
- Ambition free vision may rearrange geometry; it may **not** drop Must-see pins
