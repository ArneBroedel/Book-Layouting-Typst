# Medium rules — Pass V (Vector atomic)

## Goal

Maximum teaching with **vector** media: many small SVGs / pictograms / **legible** simplified schemas — creative and dense, not one poster, **not** caption-rescued doodles.

## Allow

- Many small SVG assets; motif set reused across units  
- CeTZ/Fletcher as **vector drawing** of relations or **anatomy-simplified forms that remain readable at ~20–40 mm**  
- Hybrid: vector base + Typst labels on clear slots  
- Creative exploration within claim honesty  

## Deny

- One full-page mega-infographic dumping all claims  
- Raster AI photos as primary carrier (→ Pass R)  
- Falling back to Pass-C-only box stacks for recognition jobs **without a serious vector attempt** — **unless** a strong R already covers that job (then document residual: vector not needed)  
- Claim invention in drawings  
- **Caption-dependent geometry** (blob + red dot, smiley face, two-line “airway”, equal circles + odd polygon alone) as recognition “winners”  
- Shipping glyphs that fail **Semantic Read** (same hard gate as rasters)  

## Quality bar (hard — same chapter as Pass R)

SoT: `domains/medical/skill/medical-graphics/references/prompt-and-semantic-read.md` §2

After **every** new/revised teaching glyph:

1. Export / open page PNG (must see the mark at final size).  
2. **Semantic Read:** Lesart · Job-Fit · Falsche Lehre · Claim-Trennung — **without** reading the caption.  
3. **Thumbnail legibility:** anatomy/relation readable at chapter size.  
4. **Anti-doodle check** (SoT table): reject blob+hotspot, smiley-droop, arc-only airway, abstract ugly-duckling dots, etc.

**FAIL → block** (redraw or demote). Caption does **not** clear FAIL.  
**medium-optimum-candidate** requires zero open Semantic Read blocks on mounted glyphs.  
Matrix: caption-dependent marks score ≤2 and **cannot** be job winners.

### What “good enough” vector looks like

| Job class | Acceptable direction | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Hot joint / site | Recognizable foot/hand outline with **localized** swelling region that reads as joint | Teal blob + red circle |
| Face asymmetry | Simple face with clear unilateral droop (laterality readable) | Smiley with one crooked line |
| Airway threat | Simplified cross-section / lumen narrowing that reads as airway | Two bars + red semicircles |
| Ugly duckling | Small field of mole-like marks with **one** clearly different lesion **on a skin-like field** | Four identical circles + one polygon on white |
| Relation / TIME | Clear flowchart with few nodes | Decorative arcs with no readable structure |

If the schema cannot hit this bar within craft budget → **residual: teach that job in R or T/C**, do not force a doodle into the optimum package.

## Exhaustion signal

Vector atoms cover recognition/relation **with PASS Semantic Read**; residual needs photo realism or pure type — document.  
Budget exhausted with only FAIL glyphs → residual-at-max; **do not** mark recognition jobs as V-winners.
