# Phase 1 — Portfolio curation

**Track:** multi-chapter-explore-layout  
**Exit:** W0 + W1 lists locked in [portfolio.md](portfolio.md); Human sign-off

---

## Goals

1. Choose **which** chapters (plurality, not catalog).  
2. Ensure **genre variety** for creative pressure (unless Human opts KL-only).  
3. Assign **slugs** (clean slate, date-stamped) and avoid poisoned prior pilots as SoT.

---

## Curation rules

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Mix form pressure (urgency-heavy, mimic-heavy, quiet KL, non-KL) | Only “easy” pin-swaps |
| Prefer format-ok / ampel green content when available | Copy old `kl-crps` / wave modules as clinical SoT |
| One clean-slate slug per chapter | Reuse failed pilot paths as SoT |
| Note ambition hypothesis in portfolio “Ambition hint” | Mandate free vision for every unit |

---

## Steps

1. List candidates from content roots (Welle-06 monographs, Welle-04 mix).  
2. Human pick/edit table in portfolio.md to N rows.  
3. For each: genre, C path, proposed slug, ambition hint (or “grammar-first”).  
4. Mark W0 (1–2) vs W1 (rest of N).  
5. Human lock: date in portfolio.md “Lock record”.  
6. Mirror locked rows onto board.md chapter inventory.  
7. Run-log Phase 1 DONE.

---

## Creativity slot (portfolio level)

At least **two** chapters should plan a real ambition unit (spatial, peel, criteria-by-picture, novel dashboard) — not only stack+mimic monotony.  
If Human prefers low-risk first wave: document “ambition deferred to W2” in notes.
